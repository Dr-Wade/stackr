## Design Context

### Users
Public, drop-in users. Streamers, hobbyists, and curious tinkerers who land on the app and want to assemble a quick browser-source overlay without reading docs. They're configuring overlays for OBS, recordings, or personal experiments. They expect the tool to "just work" on first load: an empty state that invites them to add a source, a visible canvas, and an output URL they can copy. Power-user shortcuts are welcome but never required.

### Brand Personality
Playful, confident, considered. Three words: **crafted, lively, direct.** The tool has opinions — sensible defaults, a clear primary action, a single accent color used with intent — but it never lectures. Voice is short and human ("Add a source", "Copy overlay URL"), not corporate ("Click here to configure your source asset"). Empty states have a little warmth; errors are honest and friendly.

### Aesthetic Direction
Raycast / Arc lineage: dark, modern, slightly soft. Rounded corners (8–12px), generous-but-not-airy spacing, one vivid accent against deep neutral greys. Subtle motion (150–250ms ease-out) on interactive elements. Inter or Geist for UI, JetBrains Mono for URLs and code. Crisp 1px borders at low opacity instead of heavy dividers. Avoid: skeuomorphism, neon gradients, OBS-style dense control panels, cluttered toolbars, marketing-page flourishes.

**Palette (proposed):**
- Background: near-black neutrals (`#0B0B0F` base, `#16161D` panel, `#1F1F28` raised)
- Text: high-contrast off-white (`#EDEDF2`), muted (`#9A9AAA`), faint (`#5C5C6A`)
- Accent: warm coral-pink `#FF6B6B` (Raycast-ish) — used sparingly for the primary CTA, active states, and selection
- Semantic: green for live/connected, amber for warnings, red reserved for destructive

### Design Principles
1. **One primary action per screen.** The accent color appears once or twice, never as wallpaper. The user should always know what to do next.
2. **The canvas is the hero.** The overlay preview takes visual priority; controls orbit around it without competing.
3. **Friendly empty states.** Nothing intimidates a first-time visitor. Every empty list has a one-line invitation and a button.
4. **Copy is short, human, and active.** "Add source" not "Create new source item". Microcopy explains *what happens next*, not what the field is.
5. **Motion has meaning.** Animation confirms state changes (added, removed, dragged) — never decorative. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Keyboard-respectful.** Every action reachable by keyboard, visible focus rings, sensible tab order. WCAG AA contrast on all text.
